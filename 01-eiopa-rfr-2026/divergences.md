- **Why B.** For a question that an input's label decides, the preregistration's rule is: B where the text's definition, read against the bundle's labels, singles the reading out, T where it does not (§6, the candidate at ¶9.2.5). The author's reading: the heading and the label say together that the rows are yields in pounds, already constructed — the text leaves steps 4 to 6 in pounds no other source —, so the code applies the construction a second time. **Against it, for the review:** the rows have the form of an index — one duration and one yield, repeated over the maturity buckets — where G.5.3's result would move with the maturity; the label's term is not the text's; and the build recorded its choice as a choice between two readings.
